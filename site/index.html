One number a day

Enter what you start the semester with and your big costs. Runway works out what you can spend today and keeps adjusting as you log.

Big costs, reserved

Rent, textbooks, a flight home: they're set aside before you see your daily number, so they never sneak up on you.

Saving built in

Pick a savings goal for the month or semester. It comes off the top, and Runway tells you if you're on track to keep it.

Squad, not shame

Start a squad with roommates: streaks and a no-delivery week together. Friends see your first name and streak, never your money.

Private by design

No bank linking, no ads, no tracking. Your budget lives on your device. Sync it to your other devices if you like: it's encrypted with a passphrase only you know, so nobody else can read it, including us.

Meet Mochi

Log daily and Mochi levels up. Overspend and Mochi gets worried. It's a lot harder to ignore a sad little pet than a spreadsheet.

About the web version: your budget is saved in this browser, and isn't encrypted there. Sign in and turn on sync to see the same budget on your phone and in the browser. Signing in is optional.
