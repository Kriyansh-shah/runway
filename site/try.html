Six steps to take-off

  1. 1

    Say hi

    Type your first name and tap Get started. Or tap Continue with Google if you want to sync with your phone or join a squad later. It's optional.

  2. 2

    Set up your semester

    Choose a semester or a month, set the start and end dates, and enter how much money you have for it.

  3. 3

    Add big costs

    Rent, textbooks, a flight home. Runway sets that money aside so it can't surprise you. Then tap Start my runway.

  4. 4

    Check your number

    Safe to spend today on Home is what you can spend today and still have money until the end of the semester.

  5. 5

    Log what you spend

    Tap the + button, enter the amount and pick a category. Your daily number updates right away.

  6. 6

    Look around

    Set a goal in Savings, ask Coach where your money went, or split a bill with friends in Squad.

📱 Add it to your home screen

iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Runway then opens from an icon, like an app.

🔄 Use the same budget on another device

Sign in with Google, then go to Settings → Account → Budget sync, turn it on and pick a passphrase.

On your other device, sign in with the same Google account and enter that passphrase. Only you can read the synced copy.

Good to know

  • Your budget is saved in this browser, and isn't encrypted there.
  • Clearing your browsing data or using a private window removes it. Turn on Budget sync if you want a backup.
  • No bank linking, no ads, no tracking.
