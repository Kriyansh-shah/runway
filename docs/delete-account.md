# Delete your Runway account

You don't need an account to use Runway. If you made one (with Apple or
Google), you can delete it, and everything stored with it, at any time.

## In the app (iPhone, iPad or Android)

1. Open Runway and go to **Settings** (tap your initial at the top of Home).
2. Under **Account**, tap **Delete account** and confirm.
   If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm first.

It's deleted right away.

## Without the app

If you've deleted the app or can't open it:

1. Go to the [Runway web app](app/) on any computer or phone.
2. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used in the app.
3. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account** and confirm.

## What's deleted

Right away, from Runway's server:

- your account: name, email address and account ID
- your synced budget, if you turned on Budget sync (it was encrypted, so
  nobody could read it anyway)
- your friends, the bills you split with them and your settle-ups
- your squad memberships and streak (web app)
- the list of people you blocked

Runway's access to your Apple ID is revoked too, if you signed in with Apple.

## What's kept

- **Reports you made** about someone are kept for safety review, without
  your name on them.
- **Your budget on your phone** isn't touched by deleting the account; it
  only ever lived on the device. To remove it, use **Settings → Erase
  everything on this phone**, or delete the app.

Nothing else is kept. Runway has no analytics, ads or tracking data to
delete.
