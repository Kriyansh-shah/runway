# Runway Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? We'd love to hear it.

## Get in touch

- **Report a bug or ask a question:** [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Kriyansh-shah/runway/issues/new).
  Please don't include amounts or other personal money details.

## Common questions

**Where is my budget saved?**
On your device. On iPhone and Android it's encrypted. In the web app it's
saved in that browser.

**How do I see my budget on another device?**
Sign in with Apple or Google, then Settings → Account → **Budget sync** →
turn it on and pick a passphrase. On the other device, sign in with the same
account and enter the passphrase. The synced copy is encrypted so only you
can read it.

**I forgot my sync passphrase.**
Nobody can open the synced copy without it, not even us. On a device that
still has your budget, go to Budget sync → Forgot it? → start over with a
new passphrase.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional; it's only needed for
budget sync.

**How do I log an expense with Siri?**
Say "Log an expense in Runway", then tell Siri what you spent, like "8 bucks
on coffee". It's added the next time you open Runway. Your iPhone needs to be
unlocked.

**How do I add a friend?**
Sign in, open **Friends** → **Add friend**, and share your code or type
theirs. Friends see your first name and an emoji, never your budget, balance
or other expenses.

**How do split bills work?**
Friends → **Split a bill**. Pick who paid (you or a friend), who's in, and
how to split it: equally, by exact amounts, percentages or shares. Your part
counts as spending in your budget; each friend sees the split and what they
owe in their app. Tap a friend to settle up. Only the people in a split see
its amounts. Someone not on Runway? Add them by name; that IOU stays on your
phone.

**Someone is bothering me. What can I do?**
Tap them in Friends to remove, block or report them. Unblock people in
Settings → Account → Blocked people.

Squads (streaks and challenges with a group) are in the web app for now and
come to the phone apps in a later update.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Account → **Delete account**. It's deleted right away (if you
signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm first). Your budget
on the device stays until you use **Erase everything on this phone**.

**How do I start over?**
Settings → **Erase everything on this phone** deletes all your data on that
device and signs you out.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
