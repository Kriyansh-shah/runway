# Runway Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

**The short version: your money data stays on your device, and we can't see
it. Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, only your name, email and an
account ID are stored on our server, plus your streak if you join a
squad.**

## What Runway stores, and where

Runway keeps what you type in so it can work out your budget:

- your first name, which is only used for the greeting
- your budget: semester or month dates, the money you start with, expenses
  and their notes, big costs and monthly bills, and money coming in
- bill splits with roommates, your logging streak, and your settings

All of it is saved **only on your device**, in Runway's private storage that
other apps can't open.

- **On iPhone and Android** the file is **encrypted**. The key is random,
  made on your device, and kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore.
- **On the web version** the data is kept in your browser's storage for the
  site, and it isn't encrypted.

## What leaves your device

**Your money data never does.** Balances, expenses, budgets, bills and splits
stay on your device whether or not you sign in.

**If you don't sign in, nothing leaves your device.**

**If you choose "Continue with Google":**

- Google confirms who you are and tells Runway your **name, email address and
  Google account ID**. Runway never sees your Google password.
- Those are stored in Runway's account database, run by
  [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) on servers in the United States,
  so you can sign in again later and use Squad.
- Runway keeps a sign-in session on your device, in the iOS Keychain or
  Android Keystore (in the web app, in your browser's storage for the site).

Runway has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never sell or share your
information, and it's used only to run your account.

## Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the app lock, your phone checks your face, fingerprint or
passcode. Runway only learns whether the check passed. It never sees or
stores any biometric data.

## Backups

If you back up your phone (for example to iCloud), the backup can include
Runway's encrypted file. The key that opens it is only included in
**encrypted** backups. Backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own
privacy policies.

## Squad

Squad needs you to sign in. When you start or join a squad, Runway stores on
our server and shows **only to people in your squads**:

- your first name and a squad emoji
- your logging streak (days in a row you logged)
- which days of the current no-delivery week you skipped delivery
- the squads you're in, their names, and their invite codes

Friends **never** see your balances, expenses, budget or any amounts. Bill
splits with roommates stay on your device. Leaving a squad stops sharing
with it right away, and a squad is deleted when its last member leaves.

## Deleting your data

- **Your account:** Settings → Account → **Delete account** deletes your
  account and everything stored with it on our server, right away.
- **Data on your device:** Settings → **Erase everything on this phone**
  deletes all of it and rewrites the file so nothing is left behind. It also
  signs you out.
- Or delete the app. Its data on the device is deleted with it. (This doesn't
  delete your account; use Delete account for that.)

## Children

Runway is made for college students and isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
